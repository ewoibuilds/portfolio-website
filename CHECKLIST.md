# Acceptance checklist

Your portfolio is finished when every box is ticked. Check them in this order.

## Content

- [ ] Your own name, one line on what you do, and where you are (or "remote")
- [ ] At least three projects or exercises, each with one sentence and a working link
- [ ] Skills you can actually show
- [ ] A way to contact you that you check (a professional email; never a password or a private phone number you do not want public)
- [ ] No example text left: search `index.html` for "Amina", "your-username" and "example.com"

## Structure and accessibility

- [ ] `<html lang="en">` (or your page's language) and a `<title>` that names you
- [ ] Exactly one `<h1>`, then `<h2>` for each section, with no levels skipped
- [ ] `<header>`, `<main>` and `<footer>` around the right parts
- [ ] Every image has an `alt` that describes it, plus its real `width` and `height`
- [ ] Every link and the button can be reached with the Tab key and shows a clear outline
- [ ] Link text says where it goes ("Code on GitHub"), never "click here"

## Phone and speed

- [ ] Developer tools, device toolbar, a 320 to 375 pixel wide phone: no sideways scrolling
- [ ] Text readable without zooming; links and the button easy to tap
- [ ] Your photo is about 600 pixels wide and under 150 KB
- [ ] Lighthouse (Chrome developer tools, mobile): 90 or more in Performance, Accessibility and SEO

## Git and publishing

- [ ] `git status` says `nothing to commit, working tree clean`
- [ ] `git log --oneline` shows small commits with clear messages, such as `Add portfolio page and styles`, `Add README`, `Add my photo`
- [ ] The repository is public, and Settings, Pages shows **Deploy from a branch**, `main`, `/ (root)`
- [ ] Your live address (`https://your-username.github.io/portfolio/`) opens on your phone, with styles and photo
- [ ] `README.md` has the live link, what it is built with and what you learned
- [ ] Every link on the live site works
